# HerSafety-Index

This project analyzes crimes against women in India using historical crime data across states and years.
The objective is to identify trends, growth patterns, high-risk states, and crime category correlations to support data-driven policy recommendations.

This project demonstrates end-to-end data analytics skills including data cleaning, feature engineering, KPI calculation, trend analysis, and actionable insights — aligned with consulting and Big 4 analytics expectations.

---

# 🎯 Business / Policy Objective

Identify states and years with highest crime growth
Understand which crime categories are rising fastest
Analyze long-term growth (CAGR) of crimes
Derive actionable, state-wise policy recommendations

---

# 🗂 Dataset Information

Source: Government / NCRB crime statistics dataset
Time Period: 2001-2021 (year-wise crime records)
Granularity: State × Year × Crime Type

---

# 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used

Python
Pandas, NumPy
Matplotlib, Seaborn
Jupyter Notebook

# 🔧 Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering

Removed missing and inconsistent values
Standardized state names
# Created:
Year-on-Year (YoY) growth
CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate)
Aggregated crime indicators
Normalized values for comparative analysis

---

# 📈 Key KPIs Analyzed
KPI	Description
Total Crimes Against Women :Overall magnitude of crimes across all states and years.
Average Annual Crimes (Stability Indicator):Typical annual crime volume at the national level.
Domestic Violence Share (%) of Total Crimes:Contribution of Domestic Violence to overall crime.
CAGR of Total Crimes (Long-Term Growth Rate):Average annual growth rate over 20 years.
Top 5 States’ Contribution (%) to National Crime:Crime concentration across regions.

# 🔍 Key Analytical Insights

1 . Crime against women have increased significantly from 2001 to 2021, with a major structural rise after  2012.
2. Domestic voilenec is the dominant crime category across most states and years.
3. A small group of states contribute disproportionately to national crime totals.
4. High populated states have more crime rate.
5. Covid increased the volume of domestic voilence sharply.
6.Crime paterns vary significanlty by states , indicating region-specific social challenges.
7.No sustained long-term decline was seen in crime levels in recent years.
